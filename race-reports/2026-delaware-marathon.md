## Pacing Story

**Miles 1–4: Controlled start.** Settled into the 7:05–7:14 range right away. Heart rate climbing steadily into the low 150s — a good sign of staying aerobic early.

**Mile 5: The big climb.** A 98-foot gain pushed the pace to 7:41 — the slowest mile of the day. Not unexpected; the GAP (grade-adjusted pace) was still 7:04, meaning effort stayed consistent.

**Miles 6–9: Recovery and settling in.** Came back down into the 7:20–7:32 range after the climb. Manageable.

**Miles 10–12: Downhill dividend.** Three miles of net descent (-224 ft combined) and the pace reflected it: 7:10–7:17 range, HR staying honest at 150–153.

**Miles 13–21: The middle miles.** Largely steady in the 7:07–7:29 band. Heart rate crept up into the 158–166 zone — the race was asking something. Miles 18–21 were the fastest stretch of the back half, peaking at 7:00 for mile 19.

The data tells a story: HR dropped sharply from 166 to 138 then 127, and pace slipped to 7:37–7:36 range. Something happened around mile 22. Whatever it was, I came through it.

**Final 0.75 mi: Strong finish.** 7:12 pace and HR back up to 166 — came home hard.
