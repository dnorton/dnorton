Next up is the Boston Marathon on Monday, April 19, 2027. The goal is **3:05 (7:04/mi)**, eight and a half minutes under my [Charlotte time]({{ '/notes/charlotte-marathon/' | relative_url }}).

The full week-by-week plan is on the [training calendar]({{ '/boston-marathon/calendar.html' | relative_url }}).

### Why 3:05

Charlotte was a hilly course with 1,158 ft of gain, and I ran the first half on purpose below marathon effort. By the end of that block, my sub-threshold reps at 7:05/mi were running at HR 136–137, well below where they started. A 3:05 asks for 7:04/mi, which is roughly the pace I can already hold at sub-threshold effort.

### How the block is built

| Weeks | Phase | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 16–Dec 27 | Base | Two recovery weeks after Charlotte, then a rebuild to about 45 miles a week |
| 1–4 | Endurance | Volume rises from 52 to 58, then a cutback |
| 5–9 | Threshold development | Volume holds near 60 while sub-threshold reps get longer; ends with the half |
| 10–12 | Race prep and peak | Marathon pace inside the long run: 10, then 12 on hills, then 14 miles |
| 13–16 | Taper | About three and a half weeks, with short, sharp quality |

### What's carried over from Charlotte

- **Two sub-threshold sessions a week, everything else easy.** These are Norwegian Singles-style reps with short floats. This time they're governed by heart rate, not pace, so the pace can get faster as fitness improves. Progress comes from longer reps, not faster ones.
- **A higher weekly floor.** The goal is consistent weeks around 60 miles, not a single big peak.
- **Watching the left quad.** Charlotte's rule held hard sessions until the quad was at or below 1/10. With the base build going clean, that becomes occasional check-ins, though any radiation down the leg still ends a session.
- **The fueling plan, exactly as raced.** 90–100 g of carbs an hour on every run of 16 miles or more. Boston's late-morning start adds a 30–50 g top-up about an hour before the gun, and I'll rehearse it on the week 12 long run with a 10 AM start.

### What's changing

These are defaults with reasons behind them, not rules. Any of them gives way when the data says a different choice will train me better.

- **Avoid stacking.** Charlotte's quad strain followed a run, a ride, and another run the day after intervals.
- **Mileage and intensity don't rise in the same weeks.** Weeks 1–3 build volume, weeks 5–8 build rep length, and weeks 8–12 build marathon pace.
- **Long runs top out around 18–19 miles,** judged by what's in them rather than whether they reach 20. The longest is 19 with 14 at marathon pace.
- **Training for the course.** Downhill strides from week 6 and a week 7 hill session with the descents at marathon effort prepare the quads for the early descents. Hilly long runs prepare for the Newton hills at miles 16–21.
- **A real taper.** Three and a half weeks instead of Charlotte's 10 days.

### Race plan

- Run it as three 8-mile sections.
- Cap first-half heart rate at about 152–155. Boston's early downhills will make that feel easy, and running easy through halfway would still cost the quads.
- Run goal pace down the Hopkinton descent, with no banking time.
- Take the Newton hills by effort, not pace.
