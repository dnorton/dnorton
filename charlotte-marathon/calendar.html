Norwegian Singles — What & Why

  • Runs in the Tuesday slot for Weeks 1–5 (Jul 20–Aug 23) — five sessions total, all broken-interval (short floats, no continuous blocks): 3×15min → 2×20min → 3×17min → 3×15min (Week 2 actually executed as 2×18min+1×10min, an in-session call — see Jul 28 change note below), then NS retires as Tuesday converts to standard VO2/interval work in Week 6.
  • Total accumulated sub-threshold time still ramps and settles back for the cutback (~45min in Week 1, 40 → 51 → 45min across Weeks 3–5), but every session keeps 2:00 floats between reps rather than running as one unbroken block — floats are what keep HR resetting and the session repeatable rather than sliding into tempo/threshold effort.
  • Target: sub-threshold effort (~2.0–2.5 mmol/L), proxied by HR 148–153 bpm and pace 7:05–7:15/mi — deliberately sub-maximal and repeatable, not a hero effort. This is the "singles" version: one session per day, unlike the true Norwegian double-threshold protocol.
  • Purpose: raise the aerobic-threshold ceiling early, before marathon-specific MP and VO2 work take over the Tuesday/Thursday slots in the build proper.
  • Run on the treadmill through the heat window (see below) so effort stays controlled and comparable week to week.

Tuesday / Thursday Quality-Day Assignment

  • Weeks 1–5: Tue = Norwegian Singles (sub-threshold). Thu = VO2/Interval work.
  • Week 6 onward: Tue = VO2/Interval work, Thu = Tempo/Threshold + MP work — this reverts to the original day assignment once NS retires, rather than carrying forward an earlier version of this calendar that had swapped the two (Tue = Tempo/Threshold, Thu = VO2/Intervals) for the whole block. Flagging this explicitly since it's a change from what was previously published.
  • Weeks 12–16 (Oct 2 reset): Tue = sub-threshold (NS) at 7:05. Thu = sub-threshold in Weeks 13 and 15, goal-effort medium-long run in Weeks 12 and 14. No VO2 work.

Standing Protocols

  • Heat protocol (through mid-September): Tuesday/Thursday quality sessions on treadmill with Wahoo Tickr for pace precision; outdoor easy runs and long runs governed by HR caps, not pace, when dew point exceeds 65°F.
  • PTT protocol (daily, non-negotiable): 45-second single-leg isometric hovers ×2/day, big-toe anchor cue, cadence target 178–182 spm. Rate awareness weekly (target ≤1/10); if it exceeds 2/10, extend the current cutback week by one.
  • Fueling: 90–100g carbs/hour with water-chaser rule on every run 16mi+, starting Week 8. Sodium pre-load (1,000mg) the night before and morning of the race-simulation long run and race day itself.
  • Goal Review Checkpoint (Week 9, Sep 14–20) — superseded Oct 2, 2026 by the 3:20–3:25 reset: assess whether tempo and MP workouts are hitting targets comfortably. If yes, stay at Goal A (3:05). If it's a grind, shift to Goal B (3:07–3:08) — still a significant PR over the 3:15:30 Delaware time. As of Sep 6, this is now informed by Saturday's 6mi tempo block (6:45–6:55/mi) embedded in the long run rather than the withdrawn Hit The Brixx 10K — arguably better data, since it reflects pace control under real long-run fatigue rather than a fresh taper effort.
  • Hip prehab (added Sep 6, starting Week 8's Monday lift): clamshells, single-leg glute bridges, monster walks, single-leg RDLs — folded into the existing Monday strength session, 2×/week, light to start. Added proactively given quad-soreness-altering-landing-mechanics concern; not a response to any current flag.
